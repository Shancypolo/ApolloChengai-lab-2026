It was morning when he climbed the white bell tower, taking the narrow stairs without looking down, as he had since childhood. By tomorrow, the gates upriver would close, and the water would begin swallowing the valley where he had lived for as long as he could remember. His camera had only enough film left for five photographs. From the tower he watched sunlight move across roofs, cane fields, palms, and empty roads, knowing that choosing one place meant abandoning another. Then he descended into the town, carrying the camera against his chest while the island waited beneath the brightening sky.
